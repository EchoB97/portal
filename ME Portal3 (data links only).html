How to Navigate
1
Choose a Section
Click any tab in the top navigation bar or a card above to go to a specific section.
2
Use the Sidebar
In the Health Indicators section, use the left sidebar to filter by programme. Other sections have their own sidebar filters.
3
Search & Filter
In Health Indicators, use the search bar and dropdown filters to find indicators by keyword or source.
4
Access Sources
Click any data link in the indicators table to go directly to WHO GHO, DOH publications/reports, or Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) datasets.
5
National Objectives for Health (NOH)/UHC Indicators
Filter for NOH 2023–2028 indicators specifically using the dropdown filter in the Health Indicators section.
